SQL

Task objective: Analyze the total revenue per country in 2022. The goal is to determine which countries generated the most sales, and to rank them by total revenue.

  • I used the bigquery-public-data.thelook_ecommerce.order_items and bigquery-public-data.thelook_ecommerce.users datasets from BigQuery public data to analyze total revenue per country in 2022.
  • I assigned aliases to the datasets for ease (oi for order_items and u for users).
  • I joined the order_items table with the users table on the user_id field, and filtered the data to include only orders from 2022.
  • I then grouped the data by country and calculated the total revenue using the sale_price column from the order_items table.
  • Lastly, I ordered the results by total revenue in descending order to identify the countries generating the most sales!
  • The table produced at the bottom of the snippet shows the results of the query, depicting the ranked total revenue per country in 2022. Just like Magic!
